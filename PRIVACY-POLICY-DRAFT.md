## 1. Scope
This notice applies to KONNEKT's website and Android application, including Chats, Reels, Status, Discover and Profile. It must be updated when new features, providers or data uses are introduced.

## 2. Information the service may process
Depending on the features a person chooses to use, KONNEKT may process:
- Account information such as email address, authentication identifiers, username, profile name, biography and profile image.
- Messages, group membership, message timestamps, delivery/read receipts, call signalling records and attachments shared in chats.
- Posts, Reels, captions, hashtags, comments, likes, follows, status updates and related engagement information.
- Reports, blocks, moderation decisions and account-security events.
- Device and technical information needed to operate the app, such as app version, browser/device type, notification tokens and diagnostic events.
- Location or contact information only when a user deliberately invokes a feature and grants the relevant device permission. The exact fields and whether they are uploaded must be described here before that feature is publicly enabled.

## 3. Purposes
Information is used to create and secure accounts; deliver messaging, calling and social features; show feeds and search results; provide notifications; prevent spam, fraud and abuse; review reports; maintain service reliability; and meet legal obligations. Do not use private message contents to rank public Reels.

## 4. Messages, encryption and calls
KONNEKT must accurately describe which message types are end-to-end encrypted in the release version. Do not describe all chats, group calls, metadata, backups or notifications as end-to-end encrypted unless independently verified. Call signalling metadata may be processed by the service while call audio/video is transported by WebRTC. TURN relays, if used, may relay encrypted transport packets and must be disclosed with the selected provider.
