KONNEKT DESIGN UPDATE

The responsive preview has been restyled to follow the supplied concept: dark premium surfaces, Royal Purple, Solar Gold, Sunset Orange, Electric Indigo and Neo Cyan accents, gradient actions, neon borders and glowing creator surfaces.

The six existing app sections and local preview interactions have been preserved. The design reference image is included in konnekt_pwa/design-reference.png for reference only; it is not used as a flat screenshot in place of the app.

This remains a local-first front-end preview. Real online accounts, cross-device sync, real-time messaging, video hosting and live calls require backend/service integration later.
