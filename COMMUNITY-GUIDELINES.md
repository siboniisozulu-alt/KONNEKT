# KONNEKT Community Guidelines

KONNEKT is for connecting people everywhere. Help keep the community safe and welcoming.

## Be respectful
- No bullying, targeted harassment, threats, stalking or intimidation.
- No hate or dehumanising attacks against people or protected groups.
- Do not impersonate people, organisations or public figures.

## Protect privacy and consent
- Do not publish private addresses, phone numbers, passwords, private messages or identifying information without permission.
- Do not share intimate images or recordings without the subject's freely given consent.
- Respect consent in calls, livestreams, recordings and group spaces.

## Keep content lawful and authentic
- Do not post scams, phishing, malware, fraud, deceptive promotions or spam.
- Upload only content you own or are authorised to share.
- Do not promote exploitation, violence, self-harm or illegal activity.
- Do not manipulate views, follows, likes or recommendations.

## Keep chats and groups safe
- Respect group rules and moderators.
- Do not repeatedly contact people who have blocked you or asked you to stop.
- Do not add people to groups or call rooms deceptively or abusively.

## Reporting and enforcement
Use in-app reporting and blocking tools. Reports should include accurate details and relevant context. Do not submit knowingly false or retaliatory reports. Moderators review reports and may warn, remove content, restrict or suspend accounts. Serious threats or immediate danger should be reported to the relevant local emergency authorities.

These guidelines are a working policy and must be aligned with the final Terms, Privacy Policy, moderation staffing and applicable law before public launch.
