# KONNEKT — Closed Testing Terms

**Version:** 2026-11-12  
**Status:** Draft for a limited, invited test only. This is not a public-service agreement or legal advice.

## 1. Testing scope
KONNEKT is being tested as a pre-release social communication app. Features, availability, stored data and interfaces may change or be reset. Do not rely on the service for emergencies, business-critical communication or permanent storage.

## 2. Eligibility and accounts
Testing is invitation-only. Use an email address and profile details you control. Keep your password private, use a unique password, and notify the project team if you suspect unauthorized access. Do not create accounts for another person without permission.

## 3. Acceptable use
Do not use KONNEKT to threaten, harass, stalk, impersonate, defraud, exploit or discriminate against anyone; distribute illegal content; infringe intellectual property; share another person's private information without permission; upload malware; or attempt to bypass security or access another user's data. You may report content and block accounts where those controls are available.

## 4. Test content and privacy
Only upload content you own or are authorized to share. Assume that test content may be seen by other invited testers where the feature is designed for sharing. Do not upload sensitive personal information, identity documents, financial details, intimate images or confidential business information. Test accounts and content may be deleted during development.

## 5. Safety and limitations
Calls, notifications, moderation and some features may be unavailable or experimental. Do not use KONNEKT as an emergency service. Report suspected security issues privately to the project owner rather than testing against other users or systems.

## 6. Suspension and removal
The project team may restrict access, remove test content or suspend accounts to protect testers, comply with law or investigate abuse. Account deletion and data export will be available only when the corresponding staging functions have been deployed and verified.

## 7. No guarantee
The test service is provided on an experimental basis, without a guarantee of uninterrupted operation, delivery, availability or data retention, to the extent permitted by law.

## 8. Contact
Before inviting testers, replace this section with the project owner's monitored support email and incident-reporting contact. Do not launch the test without a real contact channel.
